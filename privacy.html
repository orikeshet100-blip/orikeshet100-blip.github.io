Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Jarvis is a personal assistant used by a single person, its owner. This policy explains what data Jarvis accesses and how it is handled.

Who uses Jarvis

Jarvis has exactly one user: its owner. It is not offered to the public and no other person can sign in to it.

Data Jarvis accesses

  • Google user data (only with the owner's explicit consent through Google sign-in): Gmail messages, Google Calendar events, Google Tasks and Google Classroom coursework of the owner's own account.
  • Messages the owner sends to Jarvis, and settings the owner chooses.

How the data is used

Google user data is used only to provide features the owner asks for: showing, summarizing and organizing the owner's email, calendar, tasks and schoolwork, and - only after the owner approves each action - sending email or changing calendar events and tasks. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to build profiles.

Storage and sharing

Data is stored on the owner's own computer, protected by the owner's login and encryption for secrets. To answer the owner's requests, relevant text may be sent to the AI model provider used by Jarvis (Anthropic's Claude API), which processes it only to produce the answer. Google user data is not shared with any other third party, and humans do not read it, except the owner.

Limited Use

Jarvis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

The owner can disconnect Google at any time in their Google Account settings (Security > Third-party apps) and can delete Jarvis's stored data on their computer at any time.
